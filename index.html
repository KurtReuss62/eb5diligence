Why the Framework Matters

EB-5 documents are written by issuer counsel

They are designed to disclose — not to explain. Terms that appear standard may carry unusual conditions. Documents that should cross-reference each other often don't. A business plan may describe one completion date; the PPM may describe another.

The 108-point analytical framework was built specifically to surface these issues — mapping every material term, flagging inconsistencies, and identifying what the documents don't say as clearly as what they do.

Developed over 13 years of EB-5 due diligence and updated for the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022, the framework has been applied across loan and preferred equity structures, single-JCE and multi-JCE offerings, and hospitality, multifamily, and mixed-use projects.
